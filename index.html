Webby's Challenge Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Webby's Challenge handles user information.

Webby's Challenge is developed by the game's developer ("we," "us," or "our").

Information We Collect

Webby's Challenge does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information from players.

The current version of Webby's Challenge does not operate its own servers, multiplayer services, online accounts, or backend databases.

The game does not require players to create an account or provide personal information directly to the developer.

The game also does not currently collect information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, photographs, contacts, or payment information.

How We Use Information

Because Webby's Challenge does not currently collect or store personal information, we do not use personal information for advertising, profiling, or other purposes.

The game does not currently transmit player information to servers operated by the developer.

Meta may independently process information through the Meta Quest platform and services required to operate the platform or distribute the game. Such processing is governed by Meta's own privacy policies and terms.

Third-Party Services

The current version of Webby's Challenge does not operate its own third-party online services or servers.

If future versions introduce features such as multiplayer, online services, analytics, leaderboards, accounts, or other features that process user information, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe those practices before or when they are introduced.

Data Deletion

Webby's Challenge does not currently maintain a database containing personal information about players.

Because we do not currently collect or store personal information, there is currently no player personal data maintained by us that requires deletion.

If you believe that we have collected or stored personal information about you and would like to request its deletion, you may contact us using the contact information below.

Children's Privacy

Webby's Challenge does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the game does not currently collect personal information from players, we do not maintain a database of children's personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the game's features or data practices change.

For example, if Webby's Challenge later adds multiplayer, servers, accounts, analytics, leaderboards, voice communication, or other features involving user information, this policy will be updated to accurately describe those features.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will also be changed when this policy is updated.
